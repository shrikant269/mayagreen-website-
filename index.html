The problem

Two sides of one carbon leak

On the farm

Residue is burned, not sold

After harvest, stalks are burned to clear the field fast. Farmers earn nothing, villages breathe the smoke, and the soil loses organic matter.

In the factory

Boilers still run on coal

Industry wants a lower-carbon fuel with steady quality, steady supply and proof of where it came from. Coal power plants must now co-fire at least 5% biomass.

228 Mtsurplus crop residue in India every year
~50 Mtbiomass pellets needed each year at full co-firing
2–3 Mtpellet supply in India today

Sources: ACPET Ashoka University policy brief (2026); PelletRates India guide (Dec 2025). The missing link is logistics and trust: collecting from thousands of small farms and proving where every tonne came from.

The plant · clean by design

Electric tractors, solar power, zero waste heat

Clean fuel should come from a clean supply chain. Our collection fleet and 300 TPD plant are designed to keep their own emissions low.

Electric tractors

Residue is collected with electric tractors: no diesel smoke in the village, lower running cost, and live GPS on every trip.

Planned with plant

Solar + battery power

A solar hybrid system with battery storage powers the plant and keeps the reactor safe during a grid outage.

Planned with plant

Gas recovered as heat

Gases released during torrefaction are burned to supply the plant's own process heat, instead of extra fuel.

Plant design

Clean air, connected plant

Bag filters control dust, there is no process effluent, and machine data flows straight into our MRV platform.

Plant integration next

Blockchain-ready carbon records

Every record is already sealed in a SHA-256 hash chain, the same principle a blockchain uses. Next, we will anchor these fingerprints on a public blockchain, so any buyer, verifier or exchange can check them independently, without having to trust us.

Combined with registry-issued credits, this gives buyers in international markets, including digital carbon exchanges, a traceable link from each credit back to the farm, the tonne and the payment.

Questions farmers ask

Frequently asked questions

When do I get paid?

The same day, by UPI, after your residue is weighed at the plant gate. The UPI reference number shows in your wallet in the app.

Who pays for collection and transport?

We do. Our tractor comes to your farm and collects the residue. You see its live location and arrival time in the app.

Do you take wet residue?

Yes. The price depends on moisture and grade (A, B or C). Drier, cleaner residue gets a better grade and a better price.

Is there a minimum farm size?

No. Small farmers are welcome. Register, and our field team will plan the pickup with you.

What do I have to sign?

A simple one-year digital agreement (MoU) in Marathi, signed on your phone in the app. We do not store your Aadhaar number.

What is the 20% carbon share and when does it come?

20% of the net carbon revenue from your residue is credited to your wallet. It comes only after the credits are verified and sold, which happens after the plant is running, so treat it as a later bonus.

Is the bamboo partnership compulsory?

No. It is optional, for farmers who have spare or bund land and want a second long-term crop with a guaranteed buyer.
